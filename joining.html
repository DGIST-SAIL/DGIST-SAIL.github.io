A guide for prospective students and researchers.

What We Look For

🔥 Passion for Research

A genuine curiosity for soft robotics, wearable systems, and embodied AI — the willingness to wrestle with hard, unfamiliar problems.

🎯 Sustained Focus

The ability to commit deeply to a new problem over weeks and months. Steady curiosity outweighs raw background.

🤝 Collaborative Mindset

We work across hardware, deep learning, and human-robot interaction. Open communication and willingness to learn from others matters more than any single skill.

Existing research experience is welcomed but not required. We value your motivation and learning attitude more than your prior background.

How to Apply

1

Browse the Research and Grants pages to see if our problems resonate with you. Read the PI's profile on the About section.

2

Prepare Your Materials

Have your CV/Resume ready as a PDF. A portfolio (GitHub, projects) is optional but helpful. You don't need a perfect submission — share what represents you best.

3

Fill in the application form below. Take your time — it should take about 5–10 minutes. You don't need to fill in every field.

Interview & Onboarding

FAQ

Do I need prior research experience to apply?

No. What matters most is your motivation, curiosity, and willingness to learn. Many of our projects involve learning new tools and methods together — we'd rather grow with someone passionate than receive a polished résumé without intent.

연구 경험은 필수가 아닙니다. 가장 중요한 것은 연구에 대한 열정과 새로운 문제에 몰입할 수 있는 태도입니다.

Are international applicants welcome?

Absolutely. SAIL operates in English for research discussions, and Korean proficiency is not required. International students are encouraged to apply through the same form.

외국인 학생도 환영합니다. 연구 토론은 영어로 가능하며, 한국어 능력은 필수가 아닙니다.

What should I study in advance?

Familiarity with Python, basic machine learning, and one of: robotics fundamentals, CAD/mechanics, or signal processing is helpful. But you don't need everything — we value the willingness to learn over checklist-style preparation.

Python, 머신러닝 기초, 로봇공학/기구학/신호처리 중 하나에 익숙하면 좋습니다. 다만 모든 걸 미리 알 필요는 없습니다.

How long is an undergraduate internship?

Typically 3–6 months, flexible to your school schedule (semester or vacation periods). Interns are paid; specific terms are arranged individually after the interview.

학부 인턴은 3–6개월 단위가 일반적이며, 학기/방학 일정에 맞춰 조율 가능합니다. 인건비를 지급합니다.

How long until I hear back?

You can expect a response within 1–2 weeks of submission. The full process — form review, interview, and offer — usually takes 2–4 weeks.

제출 후 보통 1–2주 이내에 회신 드립니다. 인터뷰 및 최종 결정까지는 2–4주가 소요됩니다.

I'm not sure if I'm a good fit — should I still apply?

Yes. We'd rather hear from you and discuss together than have you self-filter out. If positions are full, we may still keep your application on file for future openings.

자신이 적합한지 확신이 없어도 지원하셔도 됩니다. 잔여 자리가 없더라도, 추후 모집 시 다시 연락드릴 수 있습니다.
